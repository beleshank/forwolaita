For Wolaita is a community organization working with the people of Wolaita, Ethiopia on education, clean water, health and livelihoods.

Donate nowVolunteer with us

Local people. Local solutions.

We believe lasting change in Wolaita starts with the community itself. We listen to families, farmers, teachers and youth, then support the projects they choose.

Every program is shaped with local leaders, and every donation is tracked and reported back to supporters.

What we do

📚

Education

School supplies, scholarships and support for teachers so every child can learn.

💧

Clean water

Protecting springs and building water points close to homes and schools.

🩺

Health

Health education, maternal care and access to basic medicine in rural areas.

🌱

Livelihoods

Farming training, seeds and small-business support for women and youth.
